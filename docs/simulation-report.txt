  Every number below is a deterministic function of the master seed. Responses are
  generated from the 3PL using the parameters the system itself assigns to an item,
  so this validates the estimator, the adaptive algorithm and the reach of the item
  bank. It cannot validate the difficulty model against human data, because no such
  data exists yet; section 1b is the closest available substitute.

  bias = mean(theta_hat - theta_true). RMSE folds bias and noise together. rel. is
  the marginal reliability implied by the SE. Bias at the extremes is the item bank
  running out of items, not an estimator defect: once every remaining item is easier
  (or harder) than the examinee, further responses stop carrying information.

  Read the two edge bands with care. Below the bank floor and above the bank ceiling
  the mean SE is OPTIMISTIC relative to the RMSE, because the standard error is
  conditional on the model being right about the items that were administered and
  says nothing about the items that could not be. Those bands are also where the
  90% interval is widest, which is the part the user actually sees.
